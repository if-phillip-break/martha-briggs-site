About the club

One of New Bedford’s oldest organizations for women of color

Our members are dedicated to education and community service. We support the educational achievements of young men and women, and serve as role models and mentors to generations of youth.

1920

Founded by a group of women of color in New Bedford to create a student aid fund. The first president was Mrs. Jennie Wilder Lee.

1939

Incorporated in Massachusetts on November 20.

Named for Martha Bailey Briggs, 1838–1889

A proud affiliate of the National Council of Negro Women.
